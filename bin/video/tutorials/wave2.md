# Flipset tutorial videos: Wave 2 scripts (free)

F3, F6, F7 and F8. Same rules as Wave 1: one clip per line (the `id`), about half a second of silence before and after each clip, say "WordPress dot org" and "Schema dot org", not the written forms. Voice and language are decided in the tracker.

Progress is tracked in the Flipset video tracker artifact. The Pro scripts are not kept in this repo.

**Note on the closing lines.** Each clip ends with "Next: ...". Wave 2 closers follow the tracker order (F3, F4, F5, F6, F7, F8, F9). The Wave 1 closers for F2 and F5 were written for a Wave 1-only playlist, so when Wave 2 is published in tracker order, re-record two short lines:

- F2 `close`: "Four views, all free. Next: the toolbar and reading themes."
- F5 `close`: "A library in minutes. Next: Docs and Features, and your own covers."

## F3 - Toolbar, reading themes and deep links

Planned length 1:23 (timecodes follow the speech: 150 words a minute, 0.5 s of silence around each clip, a little screen action per line; re-time after recording). 152 words, about 61 s at 150 words a minute, plus pauses.

**Before recording**

- A PDF of 8 pages or more, with a table of contents (outline) and some searchable text.
- The viewer embedded on one page, with the default toolbar.
- Open the page once and read a few pages, so "resume" has something to remember; clear it again before recording the first take.
- Check the exact toolbar and More tools labels in the viewer before recording.
