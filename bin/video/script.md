## Recording notes

- Say "WordPress dot org", not "wordpress.org".
- Leave about half a second of silence before and after each line.
- Keep each clip as its own file named after the `id` (`hook.wav`, `meet.wav`, ...), mono or stereo WAV, 44.1 or 48 kHz.
- If a line runs long, tell me its length and I will stretch the scene to fit it.
